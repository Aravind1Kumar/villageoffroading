KRISHNA: THE THAR ODYSSEY

4 Villages • Deep Mud Roads • Large Water Pits
Lord Krishna comes out of his ancestral haveli in Gokul and mounts his rugged Mahindra Thar 4x4.

Loaded in the rear bed are 5 Sacred Earthen Makhan Pots (Golden Butter Matkas). Your mission is to cross 4 villages through treacherous clay mud, deep tractor ruts, and massive water-filled craters without dropping and shattering the pots!
1. Gokul (गोकुल) Starting haveli & calm trails
2. Barsana (बरसाना) Clay mounds & water crater
3. Nandgaon (नंदगांव) Dense banyan marsh trench
4. Vrindavan (वृन्दावन) Grand Mandir destination

💡 Pro Off-Roader Tip: Switch to 4WD LOW (G key) when stuck in deep water pits to crawl out safely without violent jerks that break the pots!
